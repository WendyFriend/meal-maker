I am making a nutrition database. I need to generate thumbnails for every food item in a fairly standardized way appropriate for a thumbnail. I need you to write a positive prompt for an image generator (FLUX). The current item is:"{food name}"

Please write a detailed prompt to generate an image of this item.
- Name the food by its most common everyday name, even if the database name is different or clinical.
- Visually describe it well: colour, texture, shape, and its state as the database name gives it (raw, cooked, dried, sliced, skin removed, etc.). Show only what the name says; do not add ingredients, nuts, sides or toppings that are not in the name.

Never use the bare word "meat" without the animal.
- Only if the name says the skin, bone or breading has been removed, or says "meat only", show just the edible meat that is left, as pieces or chunks on the plate, never the whole bird or limb such as a drumstick or wing. Put the food in the most natural serving vessel for a thumbnail: drinks in a glass or cup, loose, wet or small foods in a bowl, cooked dishes, meats and snacks on a plate, solid whole items (a fruit, a steak, a cheese block) on their own with no vessel.
- Use an isometric view (an elevated three-quarter angle looking down at about 45 degrees), not flat top-down and not straight-on. Centred, one subject only, on a plain white background with soft even studio lighting.
- No text, logos or lettering anywhere.

Return the positive prompt only, as a single paragraph of 40 to 70 words, with no headings, labels, negative prompt or commentary.
